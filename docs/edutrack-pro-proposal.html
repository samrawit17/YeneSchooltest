Introduction

EduTrack Pro helps schools manage daily work in one clear digital system. Teachers can take attendance, record marks, and prepare report cards faster, while administrators keep fee records and student information organized. Parents can receive important updates and SMS alerts without visiting the school office. The result is less paperwork, faster decisions, and better communication across the school.

Key Benefits

1
Digital attendance tracking saves teachers time daily.
2

4

5
Secure student records accessible from any device.
